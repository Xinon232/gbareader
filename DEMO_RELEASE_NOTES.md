# gbareader V3.1 demo

**The full version of gbareader is available at https://halimj.itch.io/**

gbareader is a book reader for the Game Boy Advance with a Supercard SD flashcart. This demo lets you try it with your own books and check that your flashcart and SD card work before you get the full version.

## How to try it

1. Copy `demo_gbareader.gba` to your SD card and start it from your Supercard loader.
2. Put UTF-8 `.txt` and `.epub` books in the folder `/gbareader` on the card, or import them in the app with `Start`.
3. The start screen shows how many books were found and checks your setup: **Flashcart compatible** and **SD card**, each with a ✓ or ✗. Press `A` to continue.

## What the demo can do

- Browse your books and read them: TXT and EPUB, Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Japanese, Chinese, Korean and Arabic text.
- Import books from anywhere on the SD card into `/gbareader`.
- Change line spacing, paragraph gap and L/R page turning.

## What needs the full version

- Saving your reading position (`Start` while reading).
- Go to (jumping to a percentage of the book).

Each book also opens with a short demo screen; press any button to start reading.
